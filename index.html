Privacy Policy — Daily Stack

Last updated: 19 August 2026

Short version: Daily Stack does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on our servers. There are no servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Who we are

Daily Stack ("the app") is developed by Healthift Sport. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no login, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting that transmits data. We never see the information you enter.

What the app stores, and where

The app stores the following on your device only, in a local database:

  • Supplements you add — name, brand, dose, unit, colour, icon choice, and notes
  • Schedules — the times and weekdays you choose for each supplement
  • Intake records — which doses you marked as taken or skipped, and when
  • Stock counts, if you enable stock tracking
  • App preferences, such as whether reminders are enabled

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see below). Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your device's own operating system. They are not push notifications; nothing is sent from or to a server. If you deny notification permission, the rest of the app works normally — you simply will not receive reminders.

Exporting your data

The app includes an export function in Settings. When you use it, your data is handed to your device's standard share sheet so that you can choose where it goes — for example a notes app, an email to yourself, or cloud storage. That destination is your choice, and we receive no copy. Once the data leaves the app, it is governed by the privacy policy of whatever service you sent it to.

Internet access

The app declares the internet permission because the application framework it is built on requires it. The app does not transmit your supplement data, usage information, or any personal data over the internet.

Third parties

The app does not include advertising networks, analytics providers, or social media trackers. No third party receives your data from us.

Children

Daily Stack is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because all data is stored locally and we never receive it, you have direct and complete control over it. You can edit or delete any individual entry within the app, delete everything at once from Settings, or remove all data by uninstalling the app. There is no data held by us for you to request, correct, or have erased.

Health disclaimer

Daily Stack includes a catalogue of common supplements with typical doses and usage times, and warns when two supplements that can affect each other's absorption are scheduled close together. This information reflects widely available general guidance and is provided to save you typing. It is not medical advice, is not tailored to you, and does not replace consultation with a doctor or pharmacist. Always consult a qualified professional about what you take, how much, and when — particularly if you are pregnant, nursing, taking prescription medication, or managing a medical condition.
